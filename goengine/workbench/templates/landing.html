The Problem

Citizens struggle to know

Government records exist. Understanding them shouldn't require insider access.

What was approved

Which Government Order actually authorized a project, and when.

What budget was allocated

The sanctioned amount, sourced from the order itself -- not a rumor.

What affects their locality

Orders and schemes relevant to their own district, surfaced clearly.

Whether delivery matches approval

Traceability from the original document to the published record.

Third Eye converts authentic government records into structured, traceable information -- nothing enters the platform unless it can be traced back to an official government source and an original document.

How It Works

From official record to citizen clarity

Every step is real, running infrastructure -- not a diagram of a plan.

1

Official Sources

Only approved Tamil Nadu government hosts are ever crawled -- an allowlist enforced at registration and re-checked at every download, including after redirects.

2

Certification Engine

Five checks -- connectivity, discovery, download, stability, authenticity -- before a source counts as certified, plus OCR and Tamil-language extraction with measured precision/recall/F1.

3

Human Review

Every extracted field carries its source page and source text. A reviewer approves, corrects, rejects, or escalates -- nothing is assumed.

4

Publication Control

A district or department only publishes once it is certified, has an approved record, and its provenance is intact -- checked in code, every time.

5

Citizen Transparency

The next phase: putting this verified, evidenced record in front of the people it concerns.
