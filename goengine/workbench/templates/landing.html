Why Third Eye Exists

Ever wondered what happened to the projects announced for your area?

Every year governments announce:

🚧 Roads 🏥 Hospitals 🏫 Schools 💧 Water Projects 🌾 Agriculture Programs 🏘 Community Development

But many citizens never know:

  • What was approved
  • Who approved it
  • Which department is responsible
  • What budget was allocated
  • What information is publicly available

Third Eye helps make that information easier to discover and understand.
